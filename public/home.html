Before you begin.

Can I use MyCam directly in WhatsApp or Google Meet?

Compatible desktop applications can use OBS Virtual Camera for video and a virtual audio cable for voice. Support depends on the app; mobile calls are not currently supported through this workflow.

Is live voice instant?

The current beta processes two-second speech segments, with several seconds of delay and approximate video synchronization. Exact lip matching is not guaranteed.

Can I buy minutes now?

Registration and package-interest requests are available. Checkout remains closed while secure customer access and payment verification are completed. Your portal will show launch status.

What photos and voices can I use?

Use your own likeness and voice or obtain the owner’s permission. Tell your audience when you use an AI avatar.
