Voice for recorded videos

Upload your voice or a speaker-authorized sample. Clean speech of 1–2 minutes gives better results than a few seconds. Audio is sent to ElevenLabs.

Sign in to studio access to manage voices.

Temporary voices can also be used for one live session. One-video voices expire unused after one hour and are deleted after a conversion attempt. Failed provider deletions remain visible and retry automatically. Converted videos are retained until you delete them. Provider retention policies also apply.

Live voice and call output · Beta

Uses the voice selected in the Voice library tab. Speech is converted in two-second segments; the call video is buffered to match. Expect several seconds of delay and possible changes at segment boundaries. Sessions stop after five minutes.

If the AI picture arrives after your speech, increase the timing adjustment. This aligns speech segments, not individual mouth shapes.
